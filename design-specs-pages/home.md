# Home Page

## Goal

Create a one-screen editorial hero that introduces the brand universe.

## Desktop layout

- Full viewport hero.
- Background image dominates the page.
- Header overlay at top.
- Logo centered.
- Left nav: HOME, COLLECTION, FILM, ABOUT, CONTACT.
- Right: FR / EN and Instagram icon.
- No cart/account icons.
- No internal footer.

## Visual style

- Dark gothic editorial.
- Black, ivory, deep red.
- Minimal text.
- Large fashion imagery.

## CTA

Can link to Collection or Film depending on final copy.

## Notes

Keep the homepage more image-led than information-led.
