# Fashion Film Page

## Goal

Create a cinematic one-screen fashion film experience without loading heavy video on initial page load.

## Approved desktop direction

- Fullscreen / near-fullscreen poster-first page.
- Shared header with FILM active.
- Large title such as `THE BECOMING`.
- Short line: `A fashion film by Palais des Chimères`.
- `WATCH FILM` button with play icon.
- Minimal credits block.
- Shared internal footer.

## Technical behavior

Initial page:

- poster image only
- no iframe loaded yet
- no heavy video loaded yet

On click:

- load video embed
- open modal or fullscreen player

## Credits block

Example fields:

- Director
- Collection
- Year
- Runtime

Keep it sparse.
