# Legal / Text Pages

## Goal

Create one reusable legal/text template that works for all informational pages.

## Approved desktop layout

- Shared header.
- Centered title area.
- Small label: `Legal Information` or page-specific label.
- Large serif page title.
- Short intro line.
- Small ornament / divider.
- Left sidebar navigation.
- Right article content.
- Optional red fabric strip near bottom.
- Shared internal footer.

## Pages using this template

- Shipping & Returns
- Legal Notice
- Privacy Policy
- Payment & Ordering
- Terms

## Sidebar nav

Example:

```txt
Shipping & Returns
Legal Notice
Privacy Policy
Payment & Ordering
```

Active item should use deep red.

## Main article content

Use numbered sections with short paragraphs.

Example:

```txt
01. Shipping
02. Returns
03. Exchanges
04. Non-returnable items
05. Questions?
```

## Design notes

- Text must be readable.
- Do not make paragraphs too dense.
- Use thin separators between sections.
- Keep the page elegant and fashion-aligned.
- It should not look like a plain legal document pasted into the site.

## Legal accuracy note

AI-generated legal text is a draft and must be reviewed before real commercial use in France.
