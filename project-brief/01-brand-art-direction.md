# 01 — Brand & Art Direction

## Approved visual identity

The approved design direction is:

- dark gothic editorial
- avant-garde fashion
- sculptural couture
- cinematic
- mysterious
- emerging designer / graduate collection
- refined, not corporate
- expressive but clean

## Core palette

Primary:

- Black
- Ivory
- Beautiful deep red

Secondary / decorative:

- Warm muted gold for small icons, ornaments, and decorative marks only
- Dark charcoal
- Soft stone grey

## What the website should feel like

A fashion world / designer universe:

- poetic
- ritualistic
- sculptural
- handmade
- mysterious
- editorial
- cinematic

It should not feel like:

- Shopify template
- WooCommerce grid
- corporate brand page
- generic portfolio
- generic video embed page
- generic contact page

## Typography direction

- Large spaced serif headings for editorial moments
- Small uppercase navigation and labels
- Refined body text, not too dense
- Strong contrast between large titles and sparse supporting text

## Photography direction

- Large dramatic images
- Dark overlays and vignettes
- Red fabric / veil as recurring visual language
- Sculptural garment details
- Process imagery for About page
- Poster-first cinematic treatment for Fashion Film page

## Decorative language

Use small ornamental elements consistently:

- small starburst ornament
- thorn divider / flourish
- thin lines and borders
- minimal gold icons
- subtle footer ornament

Keep ornaments tasteful and sparse.
