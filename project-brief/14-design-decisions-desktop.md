# 14 — Approved Desktop Design Decisions

This file records the design decisions approved during the desktop mockup phase.

## Global

- Main colors: black, ivory, deep elegant red.
- Gold is used only for icons and ornaments.
- Logo is centered in the header.
- Top nav uses small uppercase labels.
- Internal pages use shared footer.
- Homepage does not use the shared internal footer.
- The site should feel like an editorial gothic fashion universe.

## Header

Desktop header:

- Left: navigation links
- Center: ornate logo
- Right: `FR / EN` and Instagram icon
- No cart icon
- No account icon
- No shop utility icons

## Homepage

Approved direction:

- One-screen hero.
- Fullscreen fashion image.
- Minimal header.
- Centered logo.
- No footer.
- No extra icons on right beyond language + Instagram.

## Collection / PLP

Approved direction:

- Use the first generated collection concept.
- Editorial and asymmetric.
- Not a standard Shopify/WooCommerce grid.
- Large dramatic cards.
- Product cards can show price or `Personal request`.

## Product Detail / PDP

Approved direction:

- Mix of first and second PDP versions.
- Keep strong first layout: gallery, scroll-to-explore, related looks, footer.
- Right-side text must be cleaner and lighter.
- No `Description` accordion.
- Short description under price only.
- Attribute list can include Material, Silhouette, Collection, Made to Order.
- Red `ORDER` button.
- Under button: subtle low-opacity `Inquiry for this piece` note.
- Keep accordions to 1–2 rows: `Details`, `Delivery & Inquiry`.
- Keep related looks.
- Keep internal footer.

## Fashion Film

Approved direction:

- One-screen cinematic poster-first page.
- Background/poster image, not visible iframe.
- Large title: `THE BECOMING`.
- Watch Film button.
- Minimal credits block.
- Video loads after click.
- Internal footer.

## About

Approved direction:

- Scrollable page.
- More vertical spacing and margins between blocks.
- Information architecture:
  1. Brand block 1
  2. Brand block 2
  3. Designer block
  4. Three approach cards, optional in final content
  5. Process image grid
- Process image grid: 6 images, 3 per row, 2 rows.
- Images should show process: sketches, fabric, draping, embroidery, atelier, material details.
- Text should be concise.
- It must feel like emerging designer profile, not company About page.

## Contact

Approved direction:

- One-screen desktop page.
- Not scrollable.
- Split layout: left content/form/links, right image.
- Left side includes form and 4 contact links.
- Contact links are exactly:
  - Instagram
  - LinkedIn
  - Email
  - Portfolio
- Layout for links: 2 rows, 2 columns.
- Removed:
  - Appointments
  - Studio

## Legal / Text pages

Approved direction:

- Scrollable text template.
- Title area centered.
- Left sidebar section navigation.
- Right legal/article content.
- Active sidebar link in red.
- Optional bottom red fabric strip.
- Shared internal footer.
- Reusable for Shipping & Returns, Legal Notice, Privacy Policy, Payment & Ordering, Terms.
