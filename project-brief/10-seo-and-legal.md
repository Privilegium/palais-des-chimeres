# 10 — SEO & Legal

## SEO basics

Each page should support:

- unique title
- unique meta description
- OpenGraph image
- semantic heading structure
- alt text for images
- locale-aware URLs

## Legal pages

Legal content in v1 can start as draft content, but it must be treated as legally significant and reviewed before real commercial launch.

Required legal/info pages:

- Shipping & Returns
- Legal Notice
- Privacy Policy
- Payment & Ordering
- Terms

## Payment & Ordering page in v1

Since there is no online payment in v1, this page should explain:

- pieces are requested through inquiry forms
- no immediate online checkout
- designer/brand will respond by email
- final payment/order details are handled manually

Do not create legal copy that implies live checkout/payment if it does not exist.
